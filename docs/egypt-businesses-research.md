## Data-quality decisions

Only company claims and contact details visible on official websites were captured. Where an official source did not publish an address, phone, or email in the reviewed page, the field should be shown as “Not listed publicly” rather than inferred from third-party directories. The directory UI should include a source link and a “verify before contact” note because phone numbers, office locations, and service coverage can change.
