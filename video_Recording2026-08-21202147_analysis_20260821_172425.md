*   **Layout and Spacing:** **CLASSIFY** and **CALCULATE** have a horizontal layout with their icons/dots placed to the side of the text (left for Classify, right for Calculate). In contrast, **CONNECT** uses a **vertical layout** with the icon placed directly **above** the text.

*   **Connector Treatment:** When hovered, the connector lines for **CLASSIFY** and **CALCULATE** are **curved arcs** that follow the circular path of the background graphics. The connector for **CONNECT** is a **straight vertical line** that extends from the top of the icon toward the center.
*   **Alignment:** The text for **CLASSIFY** and **CALCULATE** is aligned to the side of their respective icons, whereas the **CONNECT** text and sub-label are **center-aligned** beneath the icon.
